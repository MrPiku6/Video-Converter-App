Privacy Policy

App: Video Converter

Package name: com.pro.videoconverter

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Video Converter processes your videos and audio on your device, and your media files are never uploaded to us. The app has no accounts and no sign-in, and it does not include analytics or crash-reporting tools. It shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers as described below.

Who we are

This policy covers the Video Converter app for Android, published by [DEVELOPER NAME]. If you have questions about this policy or how the app handles data, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Information the app handles

Media files you choose

The app can read the video and audio files stored on your device so you can convert, merge, trim, compress, or extract audio from them. This processing happens on your device. We do not upload, copy, or transmit your media files to any server.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps a conversion history, which records file names, file paths, sizes, format details, and the date of each completed conversion. It also keeps a queue of pending conversions and your settings, such as default formats, output folder, theme, and notification preferences. This information stays in the app's private storage on your device.

Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The app shows full-screen (interstitial) ads and rewarded video ads through Google's AdMob mobile ads SDK. To deliver and measure ads, this SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier, device and app information, general location (for example, derived from your IP address), and information about how you interact with ads. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or payment details. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no in-app purchases. It does not request access to your device location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Permissions the app requests

Permission Why the app needs it
Videos and audio on your device (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android versions) To find and read the media files you choose to convert.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) To show conversion progress and completion alerts. You can turn these off in Android's notification settings.
Background processing (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, WAKE_LOCK) To keep a conversion running while the app is in the background.
Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) To load ads and check your connection. The app needs an active internet connection to run.

Where your files are saved

Converted files are saved to the Movies or Music folder in your device's shared storage, inside a folder called VideoConverter. You can also choose a custom output folder in the app's settings. Files in shared storage can be seen by other apps, such as your gallery. They are not deleted when you uninstall Video Converter, so you need to remove them yourself if you no longer want them.

Metadata in your files

Media files can contain metadata, such as tags, and sometimes GPS location or camera details. By default, the app keeps the metadata from the original file. In the conversion settings, you can turn off metadata preservation. This strips all source metadata, including GPS and camera tags, from the exported file.

How we use information

We use the information described above only to run the app's features: converting your media, showing your history and settings, and displaying ads. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

Google AdMob is the only third-party service that receives data from the app. We do not share your media files or your local data with anyone. When you share a converted file through Android's share menu, the file goes to the app you choose, based on your action.

Data retention and deletion

Your conversion history, queue, and settings remain on your device until you remove them. You can clear the conversion history from the History screen. Uninstalling the app removes its private data from your device. Temporary files created during a conversion are kept in the app's cache, and the app tries to delete them when a conversion finishes or is cancelled. Depending on your device's backup settings, Android may include some of the app's private data in your Google account backup. That backup is not stored on our servers.

Security

Your media files are processed on your device and protected by Android's app sandbox and storage permissions. We do not transmit your files. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but the app is designed to keep your data on your device, which limits what could be exposed.

Children's privacy

Video Converter is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will work to delete it.

Your choices and rights

You control most of your data directly. You can clear your history, change your settings, turn off notifications, revoke permissions in your device settings, or uninstall the app. Rewarded ads appear only when you choose to watch one to unlock a feature. Full-screen ads may appear between some screens.

To manage advertising, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings, under Google and then Ads (the exact location depends on your device). You can also review your ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, most of these requests relate to data processed by Google. For anything else, contact us. California residents: we do not sell personal information. Advertising through Google may involve sharing device identifiers, which you can limit using the controls above.
